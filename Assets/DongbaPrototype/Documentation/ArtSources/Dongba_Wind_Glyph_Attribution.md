A digital Naxi/Dongba dictionary also lists a `风` / “Wind” entry, but this particular Commons drawing has not been matched to a published dictionary plate or checked by a Dongba/Naxi language specialist. Treat its exact form as provisional, not as an authoritative standard glyph.
